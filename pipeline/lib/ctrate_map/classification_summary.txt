================================================================================
METHOD & CAVEATS
================================================================================
Source: just_reports.xlsx (column 'Report Text', 1206 rows, 0 empty)
Script: classify_reports.py   Per-report labels: classified_reports.csv

Method: rule-based. Sections split on ALL-CAPS headers (INDICATION / FINDINGS /
IMPRESSION). Findings matched by regex keyword + negation filter (~60-char
lookback window, cut at sentence/clause break). Age parsed from INDICATION
(7yo, 7 y.o., 7-year-old, 8F, N month/week/day old, newborn).

Normal vs Abnormal: report = Normal if no positive (non-negated) finding
keyword in IMPRESSION and no residual abnormality wording after negated
sentences are stripped; explicit "Normal CT of the chest" opening forces Normal.

Findings are MULTI-LABEL - percentages sum > 100%. Same for indication
categories.

Known error sources (spot-checked, not quantified):
 - Abnormalities with no keyword match fall into Normal (e.g. an impression
   describing only bronchial stent narrowing).
 - "Pneumonia / infection" includes speculative wording ("likely infectious or
   inflammatory etiology"), not confirmed infection.
 - Negation window misses long-range negation and complex hedging.
 - 397/1206 (32.9%) reports have no parseable age.
No hand-labeled gold set exists, so accuracy is unmeasured.
